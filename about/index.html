No venture capital. No sports analytics degree. No business plan. Just a guy with an unreasonable amount of enthusiasm for a really good fantasy team name.

The Origin Story

Every year, the hardest part of fantasy draft day wasn't the draft. It was staring at the "Team Name" field for twenty minutes and eventually giving up and typing "Team Paul" again. Meanwhile, somewhere in my league, someone always shows up with something genuinely great — the kind of name that gets a laugh in the group chat every single week.

I wanted to be that person. So I started keeping a running list of puns. Then the list got out of hand. Then I figured other people probably have the same problem I did, so here we are.

What This Actually Is

A hand-built (and still growing) pile of player puns, team jokes, and pop-culture nonsense, organized so you can search your actual roster and get something better than "Team Paul." Nothing here is generated by some black-box algorithm pretending to be funny — every pun was picked, written, or approved by an actual human who thought it was funny enough to include.

That human is me, Paul Calcari. I'm not a professional comedy writer or a fantasy sports insider. I just really, really like puns, and I think your team deserves a name that actually slaps.

Why It Keeps Growing

Because a pun library is never actually "done." New players show up, guys get traded, someone's last name turns out to be a perfect setup I somehow missed for months — there's always more to add. If you've got one, the suggestion box is always open, and I read every single one.
