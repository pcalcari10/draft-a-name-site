Privacy

Last updated September 2026

The short version

This is a small hobby project. There's no login, no account, and no email collection anywhere on this site. We don't run ads, and we don't use analytics or tracking scripts.

What we actually store

When you vote on a name or submit a suggestion, that goes into our database (Firebase/Firestore). None of it is tied to your identity — there's no name, email, or account attached to a vote or a submission. A submission stores the player it's for, the suggested name, and the time it was submitted, nothing more.

What our hosting providers see

Like basically every website, the infrastructure this site runs on (GitHub Pages for hosting, Google/Firebase for the database, Google Fonts for typefaces) may log standard technical information as part of normal web traffic — things like IP address, browser type, and request timestamps. We don't have access to this data ourselves; it's just an artifact of how the underlying services work. We don't collect or use it for anything.

Cookies

This site doesn't set any cookies of its own. Any cookies present would come from the third-party services above operating normally, not from anything we've built.

Changes

If anything about how this site works changes in a way that affects this page, we'll update it here.
