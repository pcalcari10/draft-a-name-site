Trade Calculator

Most trade calculators tell you who "won" based on perceived player value. This one doesn't care about hype — it's built on actual winning. Import your Sleeper league, build a hypothetical trade, and see exactly how it moves the needle on the only thing that matters: bringing home the league championship.

\u2139\ufe0f Scope of this tool This compares this week's projected points using Sleeper's own player projections, plus an optional rest-of-season estimate. Projections are matched to the closest of Sleeper's three scoring presets (PPR / Half-PPR / Standard) based on your league's settings, so leagues with more unusual custom scoring will get a close approximation rather than an exact match. Sleeper only publishes projections for kickers and team defenses alongside the standard offensive positions, so IDP leagues aren't supported yet.
